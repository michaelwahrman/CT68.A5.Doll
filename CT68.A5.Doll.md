# CT68.A5.Doll

A5 of the three MIDI-controlled caroler dolls. It is a copy of CT68.A4.Doll, which is unchanged, and everything from A4 still works. For the rest, see `..\CT68.A4.Doll\CT68.A4.Doll.md` and the earlier notes.

## What A5 changes

1. **"Hair thickness .55, opacity .60."**
   - The new default hair style is **Back, 0.55 thick, 0.60 opacity**.
   - It is also the default in `doll_export.mjs`, so the `.ass` files get `opacity 0.6` on the hair.
   - A4's solid style is still in the Style menu, as **Back, thinner, solid (A4 default)**.
2. **"Start position of camera… further back at an angle"**, and **4, the proposed start picture.**
   - The page opens from the front left, 40° round and 13° above level, a little further back.
   - The row of dolls runs away diagonally, with the green doll nearest, as in your picture.
   - **Start view** (top of the panel), or the **V** key, returns to it.
   - Once you orbit or zoom, resizing the window keeps your view. Before then, the start view refits to the window.
   - To adjust it, change `START` near the top of the script in `src/viewer.html`: `az`, `el`, `dist` and `target`.
3. **"Blink should be slower, and a very occasional auto blink can be enabled on start."**
   - A blink now takes 0.45 s: 0.16 s to shut, 0.08 s held, 0.28 s to open. In A4 it took 0.25 s.
   - Auto blink is **on** when the page opens, but rare: every 8 to 20 seconds, and now and then twice. The button or CC 34 turns it off.
5. **"Video recording seems to jerk…"**
   - **The cause in A4:** frames were taken whenever the screen happened to draw, at uneven animation times. When the encoder fell behind, frames were dropped.
   - **The new Timing option, every frame (smooth), is the default.** While recording, the animation clock moves exactly 1/30 s per video frame, and every frame goes to the encoder. If the encoder falls behind, the page waits for it rather than skipping a frame.
     - If the machine can't keep up, the screen runs slower than real time during recording, but the `.mp4` plays at the right speed with no jerks.
     - It never runs faster than real time.
     - After a slow patch it carries on at normal speed, without racing to catch up.
   - **Real time** keeps the A4 behaviour, if you ever want it.
6. **"The default noise should be a little stronger."** A new noise channel now starts at 9° (A4: 5°), and Coord × starts at 0.35 (A4: 0.3).
7. **"Need a way to turn off the display on the right."**
   - **Hide ✕** at the top of the panel, or the **H** key, hides the panel. The dolls then use the whole window.
   - **☰ Controls** at the top right, or H again, brings it back.
8. **"Start and stop recording with a keystroke."**
   - **R** already did this in A4, but it was silently ignored after you had touched a slider, because the slider kept the keyboard focus. That is fixed. Now only number fields and menus keep their keys.
   - A red **● REC** badge with the video length shows at the top left, even with the panel hidden.
9. **"Would be nice to see a frame rate."**
   - At the top left: frames a second, ms a frame, and the slowest render in the last half second.
   - While recording in every-frame mode, it also shows how fast the recording runs against real time (for example 0.8×).
10. **"Start with panel hidden."** The page opens with the panel hidden, so the dolls fill the window in the start view. **H** or **☰ Controls** (top right) shows it.

## Run it

- **Browser:** double-click `C:\Users\micha\Downloads\claude\CT68.A5.Doll\dolls_midi.html` in Chrome or Edge.
  - Or run the `ct68-a5` entry in `C:\Users\micha\Downloads\claude\.claude\launch.json` (port 8786), and open http://127.0.0.1:8786/dolls_midi.html.
- **Keys:** S sing, T talk, B blink, P spin, N noise, R record, H hide panel, V start view.
- **Arnold:** from this folder, run `kick -i exports/dolls_rest.ass`.

The browser now keeps its saved settings under new A5 keys, so the hair and noise start at the A5 defaults. Your MIDI map is carried over from A4.

## Tested

**In the built-in browser:**

- **The page:** loads with no console errors. The hair reads 0.55 / 0.60, Auto blink is on, and a new noise channel is 9°.
- **Start view:** compared by eye with `CT68.A5 proposedd start position.png`.
- **The panel:** H hides it, and the ☰ button shows when it is hidden.
- **The R key:** starts recording with a slider focused.
- **Blink:** 450 ms from start to open, 110 ms fully shut.
- **Recording in every-frame mode** (driven by a test hook, `dolls.step`, because the pane's animation loop doesn't run while it is in the background):

  | Display | Steps | Video frames |
  |---|---|---|
  | Slow, 10 frames a second | 60 | 60, none dropped |
  | Fast, 125 steps a second | 120 | 29, real time |
  
  - The frames were exactly 1/30 s apart.
  - The test saved a 180-frame, 920×720 `.mp4`. It was checked by frame count and timestamps, not watched.
- **Exports:** `node doll_export.mjs` rewrote `dolls_rest` and `dolls_raised` (`.ass`, `_uv.ass`, `.obj`), with hair opacity 0.6.

## Not tested

- **A real MIDI device.**
